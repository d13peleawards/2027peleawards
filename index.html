The Pele

Small pieces
big impact

Great work is built by people. By the ones who ask better questions. Push an idea further. And bring a different perspective to the table.The 2027 Pele Awards celebrate excellence in Hawaii's advertising and design community-and the people whose work makes a collective impact.

For more than 40 years, the Peles have recognized creative excellence across the islands. As the American Advertising Awards' District 13 competition, eligible Pele Gold winners advance to the national stage.

2027 Honorees
AD2 Hawaiʻi Person of the Year

Cale Guillermo

Agency Marketing Project Specialist

Atlas Insurance Agency

  • Read More

    Cale Guillermo has served as President of Ad 2 Hawaiʻi since June 2023, leading the organization through significant growth in membership and programming. He is a Career & Technical Education graduate from ʻAiea High School and earned a degree in Television Production from Leeward Community College before graduating from the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa Shidler College of Business with majors in Marketing, Management, Entrepreneurship, and International Business. Professionally, he is an in-house marketer at Atlas Insurance Agency, the largest insurance agency in Hawaiʻi, recently acquired by Marsh, a Fortune 500 company, and owner of KALE!, a comedic parody art brand.

AAF-Hawaiʻi AD Man of the Year

Lincoln Jacobe

CEO

6 Pillars Marketing

  • Read More

    Lincoln Jacobe is the CEO of 6 Pillars Marketing, where he excels in strategic development, creative strategy, public relations, and brand experience. A high-impact strategist for the world's most iconic brands, Lincoln leads global initiatives for Fortune 500 powerhouses including LVMH, Procter & Gamble, Anheuser-Busch, and Dave & Buster's, and more.

    Lincoln is a prominent member of the Forbes Business Council, recently recognized with an Editor's Choice distinction for his business insights. By blending the “Aloha Spirit” with world-class brand strategy, Lincoln remains a pivotal figure in Hawaiʻi's business and philanthropic landscape, and was inducted into the Business Hall of Fame by the Junior Achievement of Hawaiʻi.

AAF-Hawaiʻi AD Woman of the Year

Mei-Jeanne Wagner

Senior VP

Anthology Finn Partners

  • Read More

    Mei Jeanne brings a unique blend of public relations, marketing, issue advocacy, and event planning to Anthology. She leverages extensive experience with local and national clients and partners to advance their objectives through holistic, strategic communications. With keen insight into Hawaiʻi's multicultural community, she plays a pivotal role in developing award-winning identity programs and initiatives.

    Her deep ties to the Native Hawaiian community, combined with her strategic leadership and collaborative approach, ensure work is grounded in cultural understanding and local manaʻo, earning industry recognition including AAF Pele Golds, AMA Marketer of the Year, and PRSA Koa Anvils.

AAF-Hawaiʻi Extra Mile Award

Jean-Paul Gedeon

CEO

JPG Media

  • Read More

    Jean‐Paul Gedeon is a lifelong entrepreneur, born and raised in Hawaiʻi, with over two decades of experience building businesses.

    He's an AAF Hawaiʻi member since 2013.

    As CEO of JPG Media and JPG Hawaiʻi, he leads a multi-million dollar company with exclusive advertising network spanning over 100 venues statewide, including The Kahala Hotel, ‘ʻAlohilani Resort, Windward Mall, Kailua Town, Times Supermarket, Don Quijote, Aloha Petroleum, and HELE locations.

    JPG combines creative production, large‐format signage, digital screens, and vehicle advertising to help brands connect with communities and amplify their voice.

    A creative at heart, he's a lyricist and rapper.

    Jean-Paul.com

AAF District 13 Silver Medal Award

Brandt Farias

EVP & Chief Marketing Officer

Central Pacific Bank

  • Read More

    Brandt Farias currently serves as Executive Vice President and Chief Marketing Officer at Central Pacific Bank, where he oversees all the Bank's marketing and communications programs, He joined the bank in 2021 as Senior Vice President and Marketing Director.

    Prior to coming to CPB, he served in key marketing roles at Hawaii Pacific Health and First Hawaiian Bank.

    He is a graduate of the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa and serves on the boards of the Hawaii Chapter of the American Heart Association and the Hawaiʻi Employers Council.
